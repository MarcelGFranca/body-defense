# Scope

## Product

Body Defense is a single-player, offline, portrait tower defense for Android. The player protects a human body from viruses and bacteria. It is played with one thumb: tap an empty slot to build a defender, tap a defender to sell it, tap an enemy to read its card.

- Platform: Android (arm64), Godot 4.7.2, GL Compatibility renderer. Desktop run is for development.
- Orientation: portrait. Project size 720 x 1280.
- Look: pixel art, UI font Press Start 2P, music and sound effects generated by scripts.
- Players: casual to mid-core players who know tower defense. No account, no ads, no network.

## In scope (built)

- Campaign of **10 levels** through the body with a boss in levels 3, 7 and 10 (`levels.md`).
- **10 towers** (immune cells) unlocked level by level, **13 enemies** each with its own trait (`game-design.md`).
- Level menu with unlocking (winning a level opens the next) and best body health per level.
- Timed waves with a call-next-wave-early button, 2x speed button, pause, sell and build menus, enemy cards.
- **Three difficulties** (Easy, Medium, Hard) in a Settings page, plus reset of progress.
- Local save (`user://progress.cfg`): progress, best health, difficulty.
- Music per level (50 s loop) and sound effects.
- Launcher icon (adaptive).
- Tooling: data generator and validator, tests, bots, 1000-game playtests, analysis, wave tuner, balance documentation.

## Out of scope (for now)

- Translation: the game is in English, translation comes later.
- Online features: leaderboards, cloud save, telemetry (a server would be needed; not planned).
- In-app purchases, ads, accounts.
- iOS, tablets in landscape, controller support.
- Endless mode, level editor, daily challenges.
- Tower upgrades (towers have one level; depth comes from the choice of tower and slot).

## Ideas not started

- Bigger waves with tougher enemies (the balance pass left some early waves very small; see `design/balance.md`).
- Tower upgrade paths.
- Achievements and a codex of enemies and towers.
- Accessibility: colour-blind palettes, larger text option.
- Real-device performance pass.

## Success criteria

- A careful player wins Medium about 90% of the time; a random player about half; a player who only builds the cheapest tower loses.
- Easy is around 85% wins for all play styles, Hard around 40%.
- Each tower pays back its cost within roughly 1.5x of the others in damage per amino acid.
- Each boss lets 12 to 25% of its health reach the exit under average play.
- The game starts, plays and exits cleanly on a mid-range Android phone; tests pass headless.
