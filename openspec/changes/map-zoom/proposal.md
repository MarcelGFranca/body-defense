## Why

The player could not see more of the map or look closer.

## What Changes

- Level has a zoom from 0.6 to 2 (camera zoom) that keeps the point under the fingers fixed
- Pinch with two fingers, + and - buttons on the left edge, or Ctrl + mouse wheel
- Scroll limits, tap radius and the scroll track follow the zoom
