## Why

The interferon mist drifted all the time, even with no enemy near, so it did not read as an attack.

## What Changes

- Tower keeps a mist level that every area attack refills and that fades over 1.5 s
- The mist is drawn only while the level is above zero

### New Capabilities
- `towers`
