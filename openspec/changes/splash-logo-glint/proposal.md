## Why

The Apolo Software logo only faded in; the designer asked for a glint at the top with a sound when it happens.

## What Changes

- The logo glints 1.3 s after the splash opens: a band of light sweeps over the round globe only (assets/shaders/shine.gdshader), a four-pointed star flashes inside the globe and throws sparks (CPU particles), and a chime rings (assets/audio/common/shine.wav, set in data/audio.tres)
- The chime is generated by tools/generate_audio.py: a quick upward sweep into a bright bell

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `splash`

## Impact

- game/splash.gd, assets/shaders/shine.gdshader, assets/audio/common/shine.wav, tools/generate_audio.py, tools/generate_data.gd, docs, tests

## Review changes

The first version lit the whole logo with a star above it. The designer asked for the glint inside the round part, like a spark flying off, and approved this version.
