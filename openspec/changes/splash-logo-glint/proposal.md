## Why

The Apolo Software logo only faded in; the designer asked for a glint at the top with a sound when it happens.

## What Changes

- The logo glints 1.3 s after the splash opens: a band of light sweeps over it (assets/shaders/shine.gdshader), a four-pointed star flashes at the top, and a chime rings (assets/audio/common/shine.wav, set in data/audio.tres)
- The chime is generated by tools/generate_audio.py: a quick upward sweep into a bright bell

### New Capabilities
