## Why

Each level should tell how the germs got into the body. Level 1 (the throat) starts the series with a short story.

## What Changes

- game/intro.gd and intro.tscn: an 11 second pixel-art story drawn in a 180 x 320 grid (two people talk, one sneezes in a caricature, zoom into the throat, invaders come in)
- Session.start_level(1) plays the intro first (not for the bots with --autoplay
- the restart button in a level does not replay it)
- A SKIP button (English) at the bottom ends it at once
- Sound generated by tools/generate_intro_sound.py into assets/audio/common/intro_level1.wav (mumbling voices, inhales, sneeze, trombone, whoosh, heartbeat)
- GameAudio.sfx_volume_db() so the intro respects the effects volume and mute
- Tests and a screenshot tool (tools/intro_screenshots.tscn)
