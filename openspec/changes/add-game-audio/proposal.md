## Why

The prototype is silent. Sound tells the player what is happening without looking: an enemy being hurt, a tower firing, an enemy dying, an enemy reaching the body. A simple background tune gives the level a mood. The designer asked for: sounds on every enemy when it is hit, when it attacks and when it dies; a sound on every tower attack; and a simple background music for each level.

## What Changes

- Every enemy gets three sounds in its data: one when it is hit, one when it attacks the body, and one when it dies. All 13 enemies have their own set, so they can be told apart by ear.
- When an attack does no damage because the enemy is immune, a short "blocked" sound plays instead of the hit sound.
- Every tower gets an attack sound in its data, played each time the tower attacks.
- Every level gets a simple looping background music in its data. It starts with the level, loops without a gap, fades out on victory or defeat, and keeps playing while the game is paused.
- Limits keep crowds from turning into noise: a maximum number of sounds at once, and a minimum time before the same sound can restart.
- Volumes for effects and music are set in data.
- All sounds and the level 1 music are generated by a script in a retro 16-bit style, so no outside audio files are needed.
- Out of scope: a mute button or volume sliders in the HUD, sounds for buttons and menus, voice, and music for levels other than level 1.

## Impact

- New autoload `GameAudio` that plays effects and music.
- New fields on enemy, tower and level data for sound and music files, and the validator checks that the files exist.
- New folder `assets/audio/` with about 45 short sounds and one music loop, plus the generator script `tools/generate_audio.py`.
- The APK grows by roughly 1 MB.
- No new external dependencies.
