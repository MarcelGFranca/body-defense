## Why

Burning enemies looked the same as healthy ones, and the hypothalamus only sent a thin ring.

## What Changes

- Add BurnFlames, stylised flickering flames drawn in code
- Burning enemies show the flames while the fire lasts
- The hypothalamus sends a red wave with flames along its front in 1.1 s
- Add tools/hypothalamus_screenshots
