## Why

Text in the game bars was small (16 px on a 720 px wide screen) and some panels were too tight or too empty.

## What Changes

- Raise body text to 18-20 px and titles to 24-28 px in the enemy card, tower info, tower card, build cards and bottom bar
- Resize the panels to fit: build cards 316 wide, tower card 380 high, tower info 660 high, enemy card 270 high
- Enemy card text wraps
