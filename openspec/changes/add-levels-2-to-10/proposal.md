## Why

The game has one playable level. The plan is a campaign through the human body, from the throat to the lymph nodes, with 10 levels and a boss at the end of levels 3, 7 and 10. The roster of 13 enemies and 10 towers already exists as data, but only three enemies and three towers have art and rules. The campaign needs all of them working, a way to choose a level, and progress that is kept on the phone.

## What Changes

- Add levels 2 to 10: Trachea, Lungs, Esophagus, Stomach, Intestines, Bloodstream, Liver, Spleen and Lymph Nodes. Each has its own map, colors, music, tower slots, waves, and a short description.
- Bosses: Mycobacterium tuberculosis closes level 3, Neisseria meningitidis closes level 7, and HIV closes level 10.
- Unlock towers little by little (3 in level 1, all 10 from level 6).
- Make the ten enemy traits work: SARS-CoV-2 minions, dormant Herpes, HPV shell, Streptococcus chain, Staphylococcus resistance, C. difficile revival, HIV tower-disabling aura, tuberculosis armor, and the Neisseria split. The chain trait stays off in level 1.
- Make the missing towers work: B Lymphocyte marks enemies, Bone Marrow summons neutrophils that block enemies, and the lymphocytes can be moved to another slot.
- Draw and animate the ten missing enemies and the seven missing towers, and write ten music tracks of 50 s.
- Add a level menu with locked and finished levels, a page for each level, and progress saved on the device. The HUD gets next level, retry and menu buttons, and a two-column build menu.
- Level names and texts are in English; they will be translated later.

## Impact

- New specs: `campaign`.
- Level 1 keeps its numbers. Its name changes from "Garganta" to "Throat".
