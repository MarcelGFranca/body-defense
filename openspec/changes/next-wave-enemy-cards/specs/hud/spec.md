## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Preview cards
Tapping an enemy in the next-wave preview SHALL show that enemy's attributes.

#### Scenario: Behaviour
- **WHEN** the player taps an enemy picture in the bottom bar
- **THEN** the enemy card opens with its attributes

### Requirement: Enemy cards on the level page
The enemies shown on the level page in the menu can be tapped to open their card.

#### Scenario: Tapping an enemy on the level page
- **WHEN** the player taps an enemy picture on the level page
- **THEN** a card shows its type, role, health for the chosen difficulty and what it is weak against, resists or is immune to, with a BACK button
