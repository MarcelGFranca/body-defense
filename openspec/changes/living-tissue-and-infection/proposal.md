## Why

Levels were still pictures. The player also had no feel for how badly the body was hurt besides the health number.

## What Changes

- The map tissue drifts and swells a few pixels, the path beats and the blood dashes flow (redrawn 12 times a second)
- The more body health is lost, the redder the tissue gets, with up to 14 pus spots that pulse and drip
